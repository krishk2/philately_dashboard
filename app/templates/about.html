Disclaimer & Legal Notes

Please read before using or sharing this site.

What this is

The National Philatelic Community was built for Smart India Hackathon 2024, Problem Statement 1762 (Ministry of Communications, Department of Posts: Building a National Web Community of Philatelists). It is a demonstration of an idea. It is not a commercial product and, as built, is not commercially viable or ready for real money.

Simulated features

  • Card payments and NPDA deposits are simulated. No money moves and no card data is stored.
  • The NPDA account here is the community's own model of a deposit account. It is not an India Post Philatelic Deposit Account and cannot be used to order from India Post.
  • Marketplace listings are user-submitted. The verification checks compare photos and claims; they do not authenticate a physical stamp.

India Post content

Stamp images and release details shown from India Post's website (postagestamps.gov.in) belong to the Department of Posts, Government of India. Issuing a postage stamp is its sovereign privilege, and its guidelines require prior permission for any use of stamp images. They are shown here for non-commercial demonstration only, with each item crediting and linking to its source. Permission for this display has not been confirmed. They will be removed on request.

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for India Post. Views and content are the builders' own, and the Department of Posts has no responsibility for them.

Your data

Accounts hold names, emails, phone numbers and addresses. This is demo software: use test data. Do not enter real card or Aadhaar details.
